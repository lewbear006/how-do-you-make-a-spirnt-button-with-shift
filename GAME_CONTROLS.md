# Rent Quest - Game Controls & Guide

## Basic Controls

### Movement & Combat
- **WASD**: Move player character
- **Mouse**: Aim weapon
- **Left Click / Space**: Shoot weapon
- **R**: Reload current weapon

### Interaction
- **E**: Interact with NPCs (when near them)
- **P**: Pay rent (when you have enough money)

### Navigation
- **M**: Return to main menu (from gameplay)
- **ESC**: Back/Pause/Main menu
- **F11**: Toggle fullscreen mode

## Game Mechanics

### Rent System
- Rent is due every 7 in-game days (7 minutes real-time)
- Starting rent: $500
- Rent increases by 20% after each payment
- 3 eviction warnings before game over
- Grace period: 2 days after rent due date

### Combat System
- Different weapons have different stats:
  - **Pistol**: Balanced starter weapon (12 rounds)
  - **Assault Rifle**: High damage, fast fire rate (30 rounds)
  - **SMG**: Very fast fire rate, lower damage (40 rounds)
  - **Shotgun**: Very high damage, slow fire rate (8 rounds)
  - **Sniper Rifle**: Extreme damage, very slow fire rate (5 rounds)

### Monster Types
- **Zombies**: Slow but tanky (80 HP, $12 reward)
- **Goblins**: Fast but weak (40 HP, $8 reward)
- **Orcs**: Balanced stats (60 HP, $15 reward)
- **Skeletons**: Medium stats (50 HP, $10 reward)
- **Boss Trolls**: Very strong bosses (300 HP, $100 reward)

### Wave System
- Monsters spawn in increasingly difficult waves
- Each wave requires killing more monsters
- Higher waves spawn stronger monster types
- Every 5th wave is a boss wave

## NPCs and Locations

### Gunther's Gun Shop (Top Right Corner)
- **Gunther**: Weapon shop owner
- Buy new weapons and upgrades
- Upgrade categories:
  - Weapon damage (+25% per level)
  - Fire rate (+10% speed per level)
  - Magazine size (+20% capacity per level)
  - Player health (+25 HP per level)
  - Player speed (+10% movement per level)

### Other NPCs
- **Mr. Thornwick (Landlord)**: Top left corner - rent collection
- **Captain Hayes (Quest Giver)**: Bottom left corner - monster hunting contracts
- **Bella (Merchant)**: Bottom right corner - consumables and items

## Quest System

### Main Quest Chain
1. **First Day**: Kill 5 monsters, earn $100
2. **Rent Day Approaches**: Earn $500, survive 5 days
3. **Arm Yourself**: Buy weapon upgrade, kill 20 monsters
4. **Monster Waves**: Reach wave 5, kill 50 monsters
5. **Troll Hunter**: Kill 1 Boss Troll

### Quest Rewards
- Money for rent payments
- Experience points for leveling up
- Automatic quest progression

## Strategy Tips

### Early Game (Days 1-7)
1. Focus on killing monsters for money
2. Learn weapon reloading timing
3. Keep moving to avoid monster damage
4. Save money for first rent payment ($500)

### Mid Game (Days 8-20)
1. Buy weapon upgrades for better efficiency
2. Purchase stronger weapons (SMG or Assault Rifle)
3. Upgrade player health for survivability
4. Complete quests for bonus money

### Late Game (Days 20+)
1. Focus on high-damage weapons (Shotgun/Sniper)
2. Max out weapon damage upgrades
3. Prepare for boss troll encounters
4. Manage increasing rent costs

### Combat Tips
- **Kiting**: Move while shooting to avoid monster attacks
- **Reloading**: Reload during safe moments, not in combat
- **Positioning**: Use screen edges to control monster approach
- **Weapon Choice**: Match weapon to situation (SMG for crowds, Sniper for bosses)

## Progression Milestones

### Level Progression
- Gain XP by killing monsters
- Level up increases max health by 10 HP
- Higher level = better survival

### Money Management
- Always prioritize rent money
- Invest in weapon upgrades early
- Balance spending between weapons and upgrades

### Wave Milestones
- **Wave 1-3**: Basic monsters (zombies, goblins)
- **Wave 4-5**: Mixed types (add orcs)
- **Wave 6-10**: All types (add skeletons)
- **Wave 10+**: Boss waves every 5th wave

## Game Over Conditions

### Eviction
- Failing to pay rent after 3 warnings
- Automatic game over with eviction notice

### Death
- Player health reaches 0
- Can be avoided with health upgrades and careful play

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues
1. **Game won't start**: Install pygame (`pip install pygame`)
2. **Low FPS**: Try fullscreen mode (F11)
3. **No sound**: Check system audio settings
4. **Controls not working**: Ensure game window has focus

### System Requirements
- Python 3.7 or higher
- Pygame library
- 4GB RAM recommended
- Any graphics card (software rendering)
